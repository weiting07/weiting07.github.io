01 / About

About me

I am a research assistant in the Department of Population Health Sciences at Weill Cornell Medicine, working with Chang Su. My research focuses on computational phenotyping, patient stratification, and connecting clinical patterns to molecular evidence.

I hold an MS in Health Informatics from Temple University. My work spans large-scale EHR analysis, machine learning, survival modeling, and multimodal biomedical research.
