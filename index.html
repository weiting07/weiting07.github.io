Computational Biomedical Informatics · Disease Heterogeneity · Multimodal Data

I study disease heterogeneity through computational analysis of longitudinal electronic health records, biomedical knowledge graphs, and single-cell transcriptomic data. My work focuses on clinical phenotyping, patient subtyping, and connecting clinical patterns with molecular mechanisms.

Selected work

Research

Weill Cornell Medicine · Department of Population Health Sciences · Advisor: Chang Su · 2024–Present. Select a project to view contributions.

Phenotype-to-Mechanism Inference in Hidradenitis Suppurativa
Science Advances · Under review
  • Developed an EHR phenotyping workflow to identify disease-associated comorbidity patterns.
  • Implemented knowledge graph–based inference to link clinical phenotypes with molecular networks.
  • Integrated clinical and molecular evidence to prioritize biological mechanisms and potential therapeutic targets.
Machine Learning–Based Subtyping of Hidradenitis Suppurativa
JAMA Dermatology · Under review
  • Developed an unsupervised machine learning pipeline using EHR-derived laboratory measurements to identify clinically and biochemically distinct patient subtypes, and built a supervised classifier to assess subtype reproducibility.
  • Characterized subtype differences in laboratory profiles, comorbidities, and short-term treatment patterns to assess their clinical relevance.
Early Parkinson’s Disease Prediction Using Longitudinal EHR
JAMA Neurology · Under review
  • Developed and evaluated machine learning models for early Parkinson’s disease prediction using large-scale longitudinal EHR data.
  • Compared predictive performance across multiple cohorts and identified clinically informative features contributing to early prediction.
Post-COVID Mental Health Subtyping and Risk Analysis in Pediatric Patients (N3C)
Computational phenotyping · Survival analysis
  • Developed a computational phenotyping pipeline using OMOP CDM–structured longitudinal EHR data to identify pediatric mental health patient subtypes.
  • Established cohort definitions and standardized clinical features to support reproducible patient stratification.
  • Designed and implemented Cox proportional hazards models to evaluate subtype-specific risks of post-COVID mental health outcomes.
Mental Health Care Interruption and Return Patterns After COVID-19 (N3C)
Longitudinal analysis · Health services research
  • Developed a longitudinal analytical framework to identify periods of mental health care interruption and subsequent return among pediatric patients.
  • Defined temporal outcomes and applied statistical analyses to characterize emergency department utilization and hospitalizations following gaps in documented care.
  • Collaborated with clinicians, epidemiologists, and health economics researchers on study design, analytical strategies, and interpretation.
Inflammatory Bowel Disease Subtyping and Single-Cell Mechanisms
Clinical subtyping · Single-cell transcriptomics
  • Integrated clinically defined IBD patient subtypes with single-cell transcriptomic data to characterize cellular heterogeneity across patient groups.
  • Analyzed subtype-associated molecular differences to investigate biological mechanisms underlying disease heterogeneity.
